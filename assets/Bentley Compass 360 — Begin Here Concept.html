Before you nominate your raters

Your Self-Assessment is done, so this is the next stage. Here's exactly how to nominate your raters, followed by a few things worth knowing before you start.

How to nominate your raters
1
Add each rater, one at a time, or all at once from a spreadsheet

Go to Nominate Raters and enter their name, email, and category (Boss, Peer, Direct Report, or Other). Click Add. This only saves the record, nothing is sent to them yet.

Adding several people? Use Upload a CSV instead on the same page to add your whole list in one go, rather than typing each one in separately.
2
Repeat until your list is complete

Add as many people as you need across each category before sending anything. There's no limit on how many times you can come back and add more later, but it's easiest to build the full list first.

3
Check you've met each category's minimum

Your rater cards on Overview will show "Requirement met" once each category has enough people. If a category still shows "more needed," add more before sending, or leave that category empty entirely if it doesn't apply (Others is optional).

4
Click Send Invitations

One click sends every pending invitation at once. This is the only point anyone actually receives an email, nothing goes out before this.

5
Send reminders later, if needed

Once responses start coming in, a Send Reminders button appears next to Your Progress. It nudges everyone who hasn't responded yet in one go, no need to chase people individually, and it's limited to once every 48 hours.

Good to know
Got someone's details wrong? Fix it anytime
Typo'd an email address, or nominated someone under the wrong category? Click Edit next to their name on Nominate Raters to correct it, this works whether or not their invitation's already been sent.
Forgot someone? Add them anytime
You're not locked to your original list. Come back to Nominate Raters whenever you like and add anyone you missed. Clicking Send Invitations again only sends to whoever's still pending, everyone already invited stays exactly as they are, they won't be emailed a second time.
Why the category minimums exist
It's not arbitrary, it's what keeps individual feedback anonymous. A category of one or two people is too easy to trace back to a single person, so each one needs enough responses before it can be shown at all. See Guidelines for help deciding who to actually choose.
What your rater list does and doesn't show
You'll see whether someone's been invited. You will never see whether they've personally responded, that's deliberate, not a missing feature. Response progress only ever shows as a total across each category.
What to check if something looks off
Someone says they never received their invitation? Double-check their email address in your rater list first, a typo means it goes nowhere, silently.
A category still shows "more needed" after you're sure everyone's replied? It usually just means responses genuinely haven't come in yet, not that anything's broken.
Reminder button greyed out? You're inside the 48-hour window, it'll show exactly when it's available again.
You won't need to find this page again to remember any of this, it's always here under Help in the menu above whenever you need it.
